## Read-only authority boundary and finding

The audit was limited to the 20 sealed REV01 controlling artifacts named by the GEN-ENC-2B source-bundle manifest, the sealed GEN-ENC-2B bundle, and two upstream files explicitly bound by `authority_value_trace.json`: the P01 simulation contract and P02 geometry-volume checks. The 42-entry source bundle was rehashed with zero mismatches.

The authority chain freezes the target volume and interval, U4 caps and interface, minimum thresholds, volume-share formula, aperture-width formula, abstract bisection rule, and a nominal P05 geometry. It does not freeze a complete fixed-sector mapping for all four scientific families. Missing authority includes:

- a family-applicable fixed-passage decomposition and exact passage volumes after Boolean overlap accounting;
- fixed-sector cross-section/height/width and cavity placement rules for every family topology;
- cavity-length lower/upper bounds and a dimensionally valid volume-as-a-function-of-length equation;
- parameter/edge/ring-to-geometric primitive placement and Boolean-union rules;
- an exact connectivity graph and component-count algorithm, including RANDOM zero-edge semantics;
- derived envelope, minimum-feature and solid-load-path formulas from the generated geometry.

The P01/P02 values describe the nominal P05 baseline. They are not authority to silently extend that topology to HAND, NEAR, RANDOM or PHYSICS. Thresholds are not derivation formulas. Consequently `STATIC_IDENTITY_ELIGIBLE` is not currently reproducible or genuinely falsifiable from the frozen family parameters alone.
